CURRENT DOCTORAL RESEARCH

Can we make breast cancer screening more personal?

At the Department of Medical Epidemiology and Biostatistics, Karolinska Institutet , I develop and evaluate mammography-based AI models for short-term breast cancer risk prediction and study how a negative breast MRI informs subsequent risk.

As Doctoral Candidate DC09 in the EU-funded HER-CARE MSCA Doctoral Network , I investigate who may benefit from supplemental imaging and when longer screening intervals may be appropriate. The planned work includes external validation across Swedish and US screening populations.
